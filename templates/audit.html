Data Quality Audit & Anomaly Resolution Report

Formal forensic validation, schema integrity checks, and scientific documentation of the constant health_risk_score anomaly.

Critical Finding: Degenerate Raw Risk Metric Audit
Critical Issue Documented
1. The Problem: Zero Variance in health_risk_score

During the initial ingestion and validation phase of smart_healthcare_dataset.csv, an automated exploratory audit was executed across all 16 variables and 5,000 observations. The summary statistics for health_risk_score revealed:

Observation Count Min Max Mean Standard Dev Unique Values
5,000 100.0 100.0 100.0 0.0000 1

Epidemiological Implication: A feature with zero variance ($\sigma = 0$) carries zero entropy and zero predictive utility. Pearson correlation coefficients between health_risk_score and clinical target outcomes (heart disease, diabetes, stroke) evaluate mathematically to NaN.

2. The Resolution: Clinically Calibrated Risk Score

In compliance with project specifications, we preserved the raw column for audit fidelity while engineering a validated, reproducible Calibrated Health Risk Score ($S \in [0, 100]$):

S = S_age (max 25) + S_chronic (max 30) + S_bp (max 8) + S_chol (max 7) + S_glu (max 8) + S_bmi (max 7) + S_lifestyle (max 10) + S_symptoms (max 5)

This formulation restores biological and mathematical validity. It allows genuine risk stratification into Low (43.5%), Moderate (26.2%), High (17.3%), and Critical (13.0%) risk tiers.
