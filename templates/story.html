Scene 1: Data Integrity & Ground Truth

Baseline Demographics & The 100.0 Score Anomaly

The project initiated by auditing 5,000 raw patient records. While core biometric fields (age, systolic blood pressure, fasting glucose, BMI) were robustly captured with zero missing values, data validation exposed a major anomaly:

Critical Finding: The raw column health_risk_score was found to be identically 100.0 across all 5,000 records (Variance = 0.0), rendering it mathematically useless for risk differentiation.

Rather than ignoring the defect or inventing synthetic numbers, our research team engineered an authentic, multi-factorial Calibrated Health Risk Score (0 - 100) based on clinical guidelines (AHA, ADA, WHO). This score restores full predictive variance (Mean: 44.55, Std: 20.82) and permits true patient stratification.

Scene 2: Epidemiological Trajectory

The Age 45 Inflection Point & The Triad Comorbidity Wave

Cross-stratifying patient age against chronic conditions reveals a striking clinical pattern:

  • Young Cohort (18 - 45 yrs): Effectively 0% chronic disease incidence. This group represents a pure prevention window.
  • Onset Stage (46 - 60 yrs): Initial emergence of diabetes (14.6%) and heart disease (7.8%), marking early metabolic breakdown.
  • Elderly Surge (61 - 80 yrs): Massive comorbidity cascade: 89.4% Heart Disease, 100% Diabetes, and 79.1% Stroke.
Multi-Morbidity Syndrome

Over 1,207 patients (24.1%) in our cohort carry the catastrophic triple burden of cardiovascular disease, diabetes mellitus, and cerebrovascular stroke simultaneously.

Scene 3: Modifiable Drivers

Biomarkers, Lifestyle Catalysts & Compounding Risks

Age is an unmodifiable factor, but clinical biomarkers and daily behavioral patterns dictate who deteriorates fastest:

Hypertension & Lipids

Systolic BP ≥ 140 mmHg and cholesterol ≥ 240 mg/dL independently amplify adverse vascular event risk by 2.4x.

Physical Activity Protection

Patients engaging in active exercise (level 2) show significantly lower cardiovascular disease progression across all tiers.

Our analysis proves that smoking cessation paired with moderate weekly aerobic exercise can counteract biomarker elevation in borderline patients before irreversible disease sets in.
