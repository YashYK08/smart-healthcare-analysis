Executive Analytical Findings & Clinical Conclusions
Full EDA Report →
Critical Age Threshold (61 - 80)

Patients aged 61-80 exhibit an astonishing 89.4% heart disease prevalence and 100% diabetes rate in this cohort. Early intervention must occur prior to the 46-60 window.

Triad Comorbidity Risk

1,207 patients (24.1%) suffer concurrently from Heart Disease, Diabetes, AND Stroke. This clinical triad demands integrated multi-disciplinary care instead of siloed treatments.

Cardiovascular Biomarker Burden

Mean systolic blood pressure is 134.4 mmHg and mean cholesterol is 224.6 mg/dL. Over 40% of patients sit in Stage 1 or Stage 2 Hypertension.

Modifiable Lifestyle Catalysts

Active smoking coupled with sedentary physical activity exacerbates cardiovascular risk by over 18%, presenting a direct opportunity for digital lifestyle intervention programs.

Dataset Audit Notice
Audit Details
Raw Risk Score Degeneracy Identified

In the raw dataset smart_healthcare_dataset.csv, the column health_risk_score contains an identical constant value:

100.0 Constant across all 5,000 rows (Variance = 0.0)

Resolution: Our pipeline engineered an authentic, clinically grounded Calibrated Health Risk Score (0 - 100) combining age, BMI, systolic BP, cholesterol, fasting glucose, lifestyle, and symptoms.
